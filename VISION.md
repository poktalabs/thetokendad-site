Not a design system. Each tool derives its own brief and its own design from this. Mel answered these questions once; every downstream intake is answered by proxy from this file.

## The promise

**Remember:** *"We're all growing constantly, and at this stage of my life I have to BALANCE my family commitments with my interests: I need to become an artist who uses his creativity to build cool stuff to support my role as a parent."*

**Audience:** **Parent-first, even at the cost of hires.** Fully committed to the tired, competent millennial parent. Accepts that a DevRel/AI hiring audience might read it as unserious. The hiring audience is won by the receipts and the numbers, not by the design signalling competence.

## The direction

In Mel's words:

> I am an artist, even though I'm not very artistic per se. I just love life, and I express my creativity through technology and knowledge. I want a canvas that explores my interests (in no particular order): sci-fi, Pokémon, videogames, sports, food. It's a window to a full range of emotions, like a multi-dimensional cable tv (I'm a millenial) that sparks connections and creates life. Evolution, progress, science, tech — all for human kind. I love Pokémon so the idea of defining your team, creating your journey, sharing experiences with friends. Discovering is another important thing: art in all its forms, I love museums, galleries, and design — good design is beautiful. And I need to say it, I love what I'm doing design-wise with PoktaCare, and I love how we got started with Regen too (our design was one of the first serif + non serif that I ever saw, I'm proud of that). Lastly, the Regen project explains perfectly what I'm all about: tech for good, degen energy with regen vibes.

**This is a creative outlet, not a portfolio and not a sales surface.** Stated directly under the fence below, and it is the frame everything else sits inside.

**Adjectives:** **discovery / wonderful** · **vibrant and relaxing** · **curious** · **aesthetically pleasing**

Each carries a reference Mel attached to it:

- **discovery / wonderful** — *"think mathmagic land — an animated movie that blew my mind as a kid"*
- **vibrant and relaxing** — *"good progressive rock or synth heavy production R&B/trap/reggaeton"*

**"Vibrant and relaxing" is the productive contradiction in this vision** and should not be resolved by picking one. It is the same tension as the music reference: those genres are dense and saturated in production while being unhurried to listen to. Paired with *airy throughout*, *symmetric and calm*, and *saturated colours on dark backgrounds*, it points at richness that doesn't demand anything from the reader.

Note that **clinical** and **high-contrast** are **not** whole-site adjectives. They describe what Mel wants from the PoktaCare reference specifically, and live in the reference table below.

### The recurring idea

One theme surfaced three separate times, unprompted, and is the most distinctive thing in this vision: **wordless glyphs that carry meaning.**

> *"Mexico City's pictographic designs for public transportation (inspired in 1968's olympics)… futuristic/alien-like glyphs, icons with simple meanings used to convey information (like in the Pokémon trading card game, the type symbols and even the rarity symbols), and good signaling in museums where words are not needed (because you might not understand that language) — things like that make me smile."*

Not decoration. A symbol system that conveys information without language. Mel notes he hasn't seen many good implementations of the futuristic/alien-glyph idea.

## The fence

**Must not look like:**

- a portfolio, or like we're selling something — *"this is a creative outlet"*
- cluttered or loud
- forced or edgy
- a dated 90s / 2000s / 2010s website
- **no gradients, no glows**

**Would be embarrassed by:**

- AI slop
- **an obvious serif font selected by AI**
- bad palette selection

## Left open

Two axes are unanswered **on purpose**, and each tool fills them its own way. They are the sharpest points of comparison in the run.

**Type.** Mel's words: *"I'm open to whichever font the design tool selects, I'm good at choosing typography from a selection, not naming it. I don't want to restrict, I want to be surprised with good design."*

The Regen serif + non-serif pairing is recorded above as **context he is proud of, not as an instruction.** Tools must not read it as a typographic brief.

> ⚠️ **The trap in this pair.** Type is open, *and* "an obvious serif font selected by AI" is a named embarrassment. Those are not contradictory, but together they set a quality bar rather than a constraint — and this is the most likely place for a tool to fail. Relevant context, not instruction: Fraunces, Playfair Display, Cormorant, Lora, Crimson, Newsreader and Instrument Serif are each banned by name in at least one of the four tools as reflex AI serif picks; Inter, Roboto, Space Grotesk and `system-ui` are banned on the sans side.

**Colour.** Mel: *"Derive it — no view yet."* The PoktaCare row above carries real colour material (saturated blue, monotone white-ish and black ground, very restricted red, saturated on dark, contrast) and is the strongest available signal — but it was given as a reference, not as a palette decision, and must not be treated as one.
