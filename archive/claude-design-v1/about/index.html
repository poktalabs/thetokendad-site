The signs

Every post carries one or two marks that say what kind of post it is, before you read a word of it. Six shapes, one drawing rule, one colour each.

  • OriginWhere this started.
  • In publicWork shown while it is still wrong.
  • MeasurementA number, with the method attached.
  • PlatformThe tools and hosts underneath.
  • CorrectionA published number that changed.
  • UnlistedA tag with no mark assigned yet.

Red belongs to Correction and to nothing else, so a published number that changed is the loudest thing on the site.
